My top three Movies of all Times.

1. Twelve Angry Men

This is a classic movie that showcases the power of human emotions and the importance of justice. The story revolves around twelve jurors who must decide the fate of a young man accused of murder. The film is a masterpiece in storytelling, character development, and moral dilemmas.

2. Shindler's List

This is a powerful film that depicts the horrors of the Holocaust and the resilience of the human spirit. The story follows Oskar Schindler, a German businessman who saved over a thousand Jewish lives during the war. The film is a masterpiece in historical storytelling and emotional impact.

3. Lord of the Rings- The Fellowship of the Ring

This is an epic fantasy film that takes place in the fictional world of Middle-earth. The story follows Frodo Baggins, a hobbit who must destroy a powerful ring to save his homeland from destruction. The film is a masterpiece in world-building, visual effects, and character development.

Bonus Series


Game of Thrones

This is a popular television series that follows the power struggles among several noble families in the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos. What is most captivating is the wisdom, basic life lessons and survival techniques one can learn from this captivating series, the more i rewatch it, the more understanding of its content and context.
